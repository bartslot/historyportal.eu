The red mannequin is a placeholder for Dante the pilgrim, about 35: a lean clean-shaven man with no beard, in a long red wool gown (lucco) and a red hood; paint him in exactly his place and size, seen from high above, small and alone on the path in the dark tangled wood, lost.
Before dawn: the wood is cool, dim and blue-green, a first pale light on the path.
