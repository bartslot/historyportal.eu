The plain coloured mannequins are placeholders for people in Florence, 1283; paint each in exactly its place, size, pose and direction:
the white mannequin is Beatrice, about 18: a young woman in a long simple white gown and a light white veil over her hair, turning her head and a gentle smile towards the young man;
the sage-green and the brown mannequins are two older women accompanying her, in long plain gowns and wimples, walking;
the blue mannequin is Dante at 18: a slim beardless young man with dark hair, a long blue-grey wool tunic belted at the waist and a hood on his shoulders, startled.
