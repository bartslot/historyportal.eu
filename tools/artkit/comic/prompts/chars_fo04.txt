Two figures in exactly their places, sizes and poses: the grey-blue mannequin is Virgil, the Roman poet, an older calm man with a short grey beard, a pale grey-blue toga-like cloak and a laurel wreath, slightly translucent like a shade, his arm stretched out showing another way; the red mannequin is Dante the pilgrim, about 35, a lean clean-shaven face with no beard, a long red gown and a red hood, listening, uncertain.
Dawn in the dark wood: cool blue-green shade, a warm first light far behind the trees.
