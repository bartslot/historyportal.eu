The red mannequin is a placeholder for Dante the pilgrim, about 35: a lean clean-shaven man with no beard, in a long red wool gown and a red hood, seen from behind; paint him in exactly his place and size, stopping short and stepping back.
Across the sunlit path ahead of him lies the long dark shadow of a lean she-wolf; the wolf herself stands half-hidden between the trees at the edge of the path, grey and gaunt, watching him. The light at the end of the path is blocked.
Dawn: the wood is cool and dim blue-green, only the opening at the end glows warm gold.
