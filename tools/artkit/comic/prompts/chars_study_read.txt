The blue mannequin is a placeholder for Dante at 18, Florence, 1283: a slim beardless young man with dark curly hair, a long blue-grey wool tunic; paint him in exactly his place and size, seated at his desk, holding a letter up in both hands and reading it, eyebrows raised, half offended, half amused. More letters lie open on the desk.
The room: a small study with rough plastered stone walls, wooden shutters, no glass.
