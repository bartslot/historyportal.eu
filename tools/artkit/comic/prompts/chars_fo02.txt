The red mannequin is a placeholder for Dante the pilgrim, about 35: a lean clean-shaven man with no beard, in a long red wool gown (lucco) and a red hood; paint him in exactly his place, size and pose, seen from behind, walking up the path towards the light, small in the dark wood.
Dawn: the wood is cool and dim blue-green, only the opening at the end of the path glows warm gold.
