Close-up, head and shoulders: the orange mannequin is Dante at 35, a grown man, not a boy: a long lean face, clean-shaven, a pronounced slightly aquiline nose, a strong chin, dark hair under a soft round red cloth cap of the 1300s (a berretto, not a baseball cap), the collar of a dark red lucco. He looks down, eyes wide, holding his breath: he has just read his friend's name on the list. Behind him, indoors, a dim stone wall.
