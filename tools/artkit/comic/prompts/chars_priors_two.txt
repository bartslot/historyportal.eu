Three men of Florence in 1300 at a plain wooden trestle table covered with papers, in exactly their places, sizes and poses: the green mannequin on the left is an older grey-haired prior in a dark green gown, pointing at a written list on the table; the orange mannequin is Dante at 35: a lean clean-shaven face, slightly aquiline nose, dark hair under a soft round red cap, a long dark red lucco; he stares down at the list. Behind them a plain stone wall with heavy wooden planks. No other people.
