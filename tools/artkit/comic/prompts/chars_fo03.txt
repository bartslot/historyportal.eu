The two plain coloured mannequins are placeholders for people; paint them as real figures in exactly their place, size and direction, drawn with a fine brown pencil outline and soft watercolour, a little more detailed than the forest:
the red mannequin is Dante the pilgrim, about 35: long face, slightly aquiline nose, clean-shaven with no beard at all, wearing a long red wool gown (lucco) and a red hood with a cap, seen from behind, looking up the path, afraid;
the grey-blue mannequin is Virgil, the Roman poet, an older calm man with a short grey beard, in a pale grey-blue toga-like cloak and a laurel wreath, facing Dante, one hand raised in greeting, a little translucent like a shade.
Dawn: the wood is cool and dim blue-green, only the opening at the end of the path glows warm gold.
