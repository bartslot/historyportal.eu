The blue mannequin is Dante Alighieri at 18, Florence, 1283: a slim young man with no beard, dark curly hair, a long face with a slightly aquiline nose; a long blue-grey wool tunic reaching below the knee, belted at the waist with a brown cloth belt, long sleeves, a hood lying on his shoulders, soft brown leather shoes. Paint him in exactly this pose, size and view, alone on blank white paper.
Soft hand-painted watercolour with a fine brown pencil outline, like a character in a classic illustrated history book; clear readable face and hands, transparent washes, paper white everywhere around him.
