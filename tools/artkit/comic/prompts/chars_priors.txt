The plain mannequins are placeholders for the priors of Florence in 1300, seated at a long plain wooden trestle table covered with papers, each in exactly his place, size and pose; exactly six men, no others:
the orange mannequin is Dante at 35: a long smooth clean-shaven face (no beard, no moustache, no stubble), slightly aquiline nose, dark hair under a red cap, a long dark red lucco (gown); he looks down at the list, shaken;
the green mannequin is the prior who speaks: an older grey-haired man in a dark green gown, pointing at a written list on the table;
the brown-grey mannequins are the other priors, men of different ages in long dark gowns and caps, listening, serious.
On the table: a written list of names, papers, an inkwell, a wax seal. The hall is indoors: a closed beamed wooden ceiling, rough stone walls, tall narrow windows with wooden shutters and no glass, dim light.
