The wine-red mannequin is a placeholder for Guido Cavalcanti, Florence, 1300: a lean aristocratic man of about 42, short dark hair with some grey, beardless, sharp features, in a long dark wine-red lucco (gown) and a small cap; paint him in exactly his place, size and pose, reading a handwritten paper notice nailed to the wall, still and bitter. The white rectangle on the wall is that notice, with lines of handwriting.
