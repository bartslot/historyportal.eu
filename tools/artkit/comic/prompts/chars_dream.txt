A dream, after Dante's Vita nuova: inside Dante's small study a glowing cloud the colour of fire fills the room with warm red-gold light. The gold mannequin is Amore (Love): a tall young man with a stern, awe-inspiring face, dark hair, in a long flowing red-gold cloak, not a child and without wings or bow; paint him in exactly his place, size and pose. In his raised right hand he holds a simple stylised heart wrapped in a small flame, glowing. The crimson shape resting across his left arm is Beatrice, asleep, wrapped in a crimson cloth: only her calm face and dark hair show. Symbolic and gentle, no blood, nothing frightening.
