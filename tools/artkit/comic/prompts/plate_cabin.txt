The commander's cabin of a small Dutch VOC ship in 1642, at sea: a low ceiling of heavy wooden deck beams, walls of old weathered planks with the ship's curved wooden frames, three small stern windows with little leaded glass panes and open sea outside, a sturdy wooden chart table with a large hand-drawn sea chart, brass dividers, a rolled chart, an inkwell and a journal, a hanging ship's lantern, a wooden sea chest, a worn plank floor.
