SETTING: Ravenna, September 1321 Night, a narrow upstairs room in Ravenna lit by one candle. An empty writing desk, a quill laid down, stacks of loose pages. A reed mat hangs on the plaster wall behind the desk. Two young men lean over the pages, one in a turquoise cloak, one in ochre. The camera drifts from the candle to their hands. NARRATOR: Ravenna, September 1321. A poet has just died in this little room. His name was Dante Alighieri, and he was fifty-six years old. NARRATOR: For twenty years he wrote one enormous poem, as long as a book: a hundred chapters about a journey through Hell, Purgatory and Paradise. NARRATOR: In the weeks that follow, his sons Jacopo and Pietro search his papers. The poem is not finished. Thirteen chapters are missing: the whole ending. JACOPO: The ending is gone. Where did Father put it? NARRATOR: Where are those last pages? To find out, we first need to know who this man was.
